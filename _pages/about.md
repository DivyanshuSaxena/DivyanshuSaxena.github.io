I am a postdoctoral scholar in the [NetSys Lab](https://netsys.cs.berkeley.edu/){:target="_blank"} at [University of California, Berkeley](https://berkeley.edu){:target="_blank"}, where I am hosted by [Prof. Rishabh Iyer](https://rishabh246.github.io/){:target="_blank"}.
I am broadly interested in Systems and Networking, with a focus on building robust, adaptive, and performant systems using agentic and learning-based techniques.

Previously, I was a graduate student at [University of Texas at Austin](https://cs.utexas.edu){:target="_blank"}, where I was advised by [Prof. Aditya Akella](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~akella/) as a part of the [UT Networked Systems Lab](https://utns.cs.utexas.edu/){:target="_blank"} and the [LDOS Project](https://ldos.utexas.edu/){:target="_blank"}.
My Ph.D. thesis was on designing abstractions and interfaces for computer systems so that operators can realize complex performance requirements under dynamic contexts.

Even before that, I was an undergraduate student at the [Indian Institute of Technology, Delhi](https://home.iitd.ac.in/){:target="_blank"} majoring in [Computer Science and Engineering](https://cse.iitd.ac.in){:target="_blank"} where I met and made friends with some absolutely amazing and competent people. I hail from [Lucknow](https://goo.gl/maps/yNiHPh9WgNbjwPeH9){:target="_blank"}, a city in Northern India.
